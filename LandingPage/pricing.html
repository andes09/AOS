Common questions.

How do you count team size?

Team size is the number of developers who actively participate in sprints — not every Jira user on your account. Stakeholders, PMs, and QA who observe but don't commit to sprint work don't count.

Do I need to migrate away from Jira?

No. Omada connects on top of your existing Jira setup. It reads your sprint data and writes plans back to Jira — your team never leaves Jira for day-to-day work. No migration, no plugin stack, no admin setup beyond connecting your board.

What happens when we grow out of a tier?

You'll move to the next tier at your next billing cycle. We'll notify you before anything changes. Annual plans are prorated if you upgrade mid-year.

Can individual developers see each other's velocity data?

No. By default, individual velocity data is private to each developer. Team leads see aggregate team-level trends, not individual performance breakdowns. Admins can adjust visibility settings, but surveillance mode is not the default — and we'd push back if you asked for it.

Is there a free trial on paid plans?

Yes — all paid plans include a 14-day free trial, no card required. You can also stay on the Free plan indefinitely if your team is under 5 developers.

What's the difference between monthly and annual billing?

Annual billing saves you roughly 20% compared to month-to-month. Both options are available on Starter, Team, and Growth. We don't require annual commitments for any plan — pay monthly if you'd rather keep flexibility during beta.
